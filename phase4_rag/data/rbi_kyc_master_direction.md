## Purpose
Issued under the Prevention of Money-Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002 and PML Rules, 2005,
to prevent banks/financial institutions from being used for money laundering (ML) or
terrorist financing (TF), in line with India's commitment to FATF standards.

## Officially Valid Documents (OVD) for identity
Passport, driving licence, proof of possession of Aadhaar number, Voter's ID (Election
Commission), NREGA job card (signed by a State Government officer), and the National
Population Register letter with name/address.

If the OVD lacks a current address, these are accepted for proof of address:
utility bill (≤2 months old: electricity, telephone, postpaid mobile, piped gas, water),
property/municipal tax receipt, pension payment order with address, or an employer's
official accommodation allotment letter (Govt bodies, PSUs, scheduled banks, listed
companies). Updated-address OVD must be submitted within 3 months of using one of these.

## Customer Due Diligence (CDD) — when required
- Opening an account-based relationship
- International money transfers for non-account holders
- Doubt about authenticity of previously obtained ID data
- Selling third-party products / reloading prepaid or travel cards over ₹50,000
- Walk-in (non-account) customer transactions of ₹50,000+ (single or connected series)
- Suspected structuring of transactions to stay under the ₹50,000 threshold

## What individuals must provide
- Aadhaar number (or proof of possession) OR another OVD/equivalent e-document, AND
- PAN (or Form No. 60 if no PAN), AND
- Other documents on business/financial status as the bank may require

## Small Accounts (simplified KYC, no full ID required)
A "Small Account" can be opened with relaxed documentation, subject to:
- Aggregate annual credits ≤ ₹1,00,000
- Aggregate monthly withdrawals/transfers ≤ ₹10,000
- Balance at any time ≤ ₹50,000
- Self-attested photograph + bank officer certification of signature/thumbprint
- Only at CBS-linked branches (or manually monitored branches)
- Operational for 12 months, extendable another 12 months if the holder shows proof of applying for a full OVD
- Foreign remittances not allowed until full KYC is completed

## Video-based Customer Identification (V-CIP)
An RBI-recognized alternative to face-to-face verification using live audio-video
interaction, facial recognition, Aadhaar/OVD capture, and geo-tagging. Treated on par
with in-person verification when done to RBI's technical standards (India-hosted data,
end-to-end encryption, liveness detection, no printed e-documents, etc.).

## Risk categorisation & monitoring
Customers are categorised low/medium/high risk based on identity, financial status,
business nature, geography, product/channel used, and transaction types. Risk category
is kept confidential from the customer. High-risk accounts get more frequent/intensive
monitoring; review of risk categorisation happens at least every 6 months.

## Periodic KYC updation cycle
- High-risk customers: at least once every 2 years
- Medium-risk customers: at least once every 8 years
- Low-risk customers: at least once every 10 years
Banks must send advance notices before the due date and reminders after, via letter and
other channels, before "temporarily ceasing operations" on non-compliant accounts.

## Beneficial Owner identification (for entities, not individuals)
- Company: natural person(s) with >10% controlling ownership/shares/profits, or who
  otherwise control management (e.g., via director appointment rights)
- Partnership firm: natural person(s) with >10% capital/profit entitlement or control
- Unincorporated association/body of individuals: >15% interest, or senior managing
  official if no such person is identified
- Trust: author, trustee, beneficiaries with ≥10% interest, and anyone with ultimate
  effective control

## Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)
Banks may bank PEPs (or their family/close associates) only with: enhanced due
diligence, established source of funds/wealth, senior-management approval to open
the account, and ongoing enhanced monitoring.

## Record-keeping
- Transaction records: retained at least 5 years from the date of the transaction
- Customer identification records: retained at least 5 years AFTER the relationship ends
- Records must be swiftly available to competent authorities on request

## Secrecy / confidentiality obligations
Customer information is confidential and cannot be shared for cross-selling or other
purposes without the customer's express consent, EXCEPT where:
1. Disclosure is compelled by law
2. There is a public-interest duty to disclose
3. The bank's own interest requires disclosure
4. The customer has given express or implied consent

## Money Mules
Banks must monitor for accounts used to launder proceeds of fraud (e.g., phishing,
identity theft) via recruited third parties ("money mules") and file Suspicious
Transaction Reports (STRs) with FIU-IND when detected. A bank found to have an
unreported money-mule account is deemed non-compliant with these Directions.

## Unique Customer Identification Code (UCIC)
Every individual customer gets one UCIC across their relationship with a bank —
opening an additional account/product with an already-KYC-compliant bank does not
require a fresh identification exercise for that customer.
