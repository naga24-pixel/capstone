## Claims settlement (depositor-facing)
- **What is claims settlement?** On cancellation of a bank's registration as insured, DICGC is liable to pay every depositor the eligible amount (subject to the insurance cap) based on deposits/loans as on the cut-off date.
- **Who prepares the claim list?** The Liquidator appointed by the Registrar of Co-operative Societies (RCS) or Central RCS, representing the interests of all creditors.
- **Cut-off date:** The date of de-registration of the bank as an insured bank. Claim amounts (including interest, after set-off and clubbing of same-capacity deposits) are computed as on this date.
- **Timeframe for liquidator to submit claim list:** No later than 3 months from the date the liquidator assumes charge.
- **Payment process:** DICGC pays the liquidator up to ₹5 lakh per depositor after set-off and clubbing. If the bank has liquid funds, the liquidator pays out of those funds first (accounted as DICGC funds released, with simultaneous repayment to DICGC); otherwise DICGC releases part/full payment directly.
- **Undisbursed amounts:** Must be refunded to DICGC within 15 days after 4 months from the date DICGC released the funds, along with a list of depositors/claim numbers, emailed to dicgc@rbi.org.in.

## Premium payment (bank-facing, useful for policy context)
- Premium is based on total deposits as of the last day of the preceding half-year; banks get a 2-month window to pay.
- Penal interest for late payment: Bank Rate + 8% per annum from the start of the half-year until payment.
- Banks making false statements or omitting material info: punishable with imprisonment up to 3 years plus fine (DICGC Act Section 47(1)).
- Failure to furnish required statements/documents: penalty up to ₹1,50,000 per failure, plus ₹7,500/day for continuing failure (Section 47(2)).
